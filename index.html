Why we’re here

A healthier relationship with the scroll starts with a pause.

Scroll With Intention is a social media awareness project created to start honest conversations about how online spaces shape our time, attention, and wellbeing.

We’re not here to shame anyone or say social media is all good or all bad. Our goal is to share practical ideas, encourage thoughtful choices, and help people feel more in control of their digital lives.

“Awareness isn’t about logging off forever. It’s about choosing how you show up.”OUR PROJECT IN A SENTENCE

A little more awareness

Make room for what matters to you.

Every person’s experience is different. Noticing your own patterns can help you decide what feels right.

◷

Notice your time

Check in with how long you’re online and whether that time matches what you meant to do.

♡

Curate your feed

Follow accounts that inform, encourage, or delight you. Mute or unfollow what doesn’t feel helpful.

◎

Protect your attention

Small boundaries—like quiet notifications or screen-free moments—can make a meaningful difference.

Try one today

Mindful habits, made simple.

You don’t need a total reset. Start with one small choice that works for you.

✓
Pause before you open an appAsk yourself what you came to do.
✓
Set a gentle stopping pointChoose a time or task to wrap up your scroll.
✓
Make space for offline momentsGive meals, rest, or conversations your full attention.

Keep the conversation going

Awareness grows when we share it.

Talk with a friend about what you enjoy online and what boundaries help you feel your best. There’s no one-size-fits-all approach—your choices can evolve with you.
